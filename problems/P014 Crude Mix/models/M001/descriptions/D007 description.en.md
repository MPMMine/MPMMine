# Refinery Blend Optimization Challenge

This scenario involves optimizing the operation of a petroleum refining facility, focusing on determining the most
advantageous mixture of crude oil sources to produce a range of marketable fuel products. The refinery utilizes a
diverse “blend slate,” a carefully selected combination of various crude oil grades.

Each crude oil grade possesses distinct characteristics, including its composition and the resulting yield of finished
products like gasoline, jet fuel, and heating oil, alongside associated operational costs. Different grades also have
varying price points and require differing levels of labor and energy for processing.

The refinery manager must decide which crude oil sources to incorporate into the daily production schedule, and in what
quantities. This decision is complex due to competing demands and constraints. Excessive use of low-quality, high-sulfur
crudes could lead to breaches in environmental regulations concerning sulfur emissions or strain the refinery's
operational capacity. Conversely, relying solely on high-quality crudes might exceed financial limitations or fail to
meet required production targets for specific product categories.

The objective is to identify the optimal blend quantity for each available crude oil source, maximizing the refinery's
overall profit. Profit is calculated by subtracting the total cost of raw crudes from the total revenue generated by the
sale of finished products (Gasoline, Jet Fuel, and Heating Oil). This process needs to adhere to multiple restrictions:

* **Resource Boundaries:** The quantity of each crude oil source and the refinery’s total processing capacity must be
  respected.
* **Operational Limits:** The labor hours employed and the daily expenditure on procuring crude oil must remain within
  established thresholds.
* **Regulatory Compliance:** Emissions of sulfur must remain below permitted levels, alongside ensuring the stability of
  the final product blend.
* **Market Commitments:** Minimum production volumes for designated high-priority product contracts must be met.

The optimal blend strategy will maximize the refinery’s profitability while meeting all these operational, financial,
environmental, and market-related considerations.
