# Refinery Product Formulation Challenge

This scenario simulates a petroleum refining operation focused on maximizing profitability. The refinery’s production
process begins with a diverse selection of crude oil grades, known as the Crude Slate. Each crude possesses distinct
characteristics, ranging from "dirty" and high-sulfur varieties to "clean" and low-sulfur options, impacting both cost
and product yield.

The refinery manager must strategically determine which crude oils to purchase and in what quantities, considering their
market prices, availability, and the refinery’s operational constraints. The goal is to establish the optimal Crude
Slate that generates the highest total profit. This profit is calculated by subtracting the combined cost of the
selected crudes from the total revenue obtained from finished products like Gasoline, Jet Fuel, and Heating Oil.

Several constraints must be considered during the optimization process. These include limitations on the total
distillation capacity of the refinery, a daily labor hour budget, and a maximum allowable sulfur emission level.
Furthermore, the refinery is obligated to fulfill minimum production agreements for key products. The solution must
simultaneously adhere to supply limits for each crude, maintain overall operational efficiency, respect environmental
regulations, and meet contractual demands. The resultant optimal blend must maximize net profit while satisfying these
competing requirements.
