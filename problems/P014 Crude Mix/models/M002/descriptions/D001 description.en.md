## Crude mix Problem

A refinery needs to determine the optimal quantity of $n$ different types of crude oil to process into three distinct refined products—Gasoline, Jet Fuel, and Heating Oil—in order to maximize total profit. 
Profit is calculated as the total revenue generated from selling the produced refined products minus the total processing costs across all used crude oils.
The amount of each crude oil processed cannot exceed its maximum available supply.
The sum of all crude oils processed across the refinery cannot exceed a total overall capacity limit.
Processing each crude oil consumes specific amounts of resources: Sulfur, Labor, and Budget (representing processing cost per unit). The total consumption of each resource across all processed crude oils must stay within its specified maximum resource capacity limit.
Refining crude oils yields specific amounts of Gasoline, Jet Fuel, and Heating Oil. The total production of each refined product across all processed crude oils must meet or exceed a required minimum demand.
At least 15% of the total crude oil processed must consist of the first crude oil type.
The amount of any individual crude oil processed must be non-negative and cannot exceed 100,000 units.
