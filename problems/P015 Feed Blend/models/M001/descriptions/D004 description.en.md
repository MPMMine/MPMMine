# Cattle Feed Blend Optimization

Consider a scenario for formulating a cattle feed mixture using various components that each contain multiple
nutritional elements. The final blend must have a fixed total mass, and for each nutrient, the total amount in the blend
must be between a minimum and maximum specified value. A designated subset of the ingredients, termed grains, must sum
to at least a certain percentage of the total mass. The objective is to minimize the overall cost of the mixture.
