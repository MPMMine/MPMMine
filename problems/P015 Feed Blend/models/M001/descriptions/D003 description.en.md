# Cattle Feed Mixture

The problem involves creating a feed mixture for cattle using N distinct raw materials, each containing M different
nutrients. The total weight of the mixture must be a specific target value. For each nutrient, the resulting mixture
must meet a minimum and maximum concentration requirement. A designated subset of ingredients, referred to as grains,
must collectively constitute at least 20% of the final mixture's weight. The objective is to find the combination of
ingredients that achieves this while minimizing the overall cost.
