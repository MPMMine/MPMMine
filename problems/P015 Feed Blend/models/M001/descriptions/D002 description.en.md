# Feed Blend Formulation

Consider the challenge of formulating a cattle feed mixture from a collection of components, each possessing multiple
nutritional aspects. The final blend must achieve a specified mass, and for each nutritional aspect, the total content
must lie between established lower and upper limits. A designated subset of components, referred to as the grain
components, must contribute at least a given minimum fraction of the total mixture mass. The objective is to determine
the optimal combination that minimizes the overall expense.
