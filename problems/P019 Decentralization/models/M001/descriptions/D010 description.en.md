# Decentralization  

A large company intends to shift some of its departments out of the Host City. Gains from relocation (cheaper housing, governmental incentives, easier hiring, etc.) have been assigned values. Conversely, higher communication costs arise between departments, also quantified for every possible site pair. The task is to choose a site for each department that minimizes overall yearly cost.  

The constraint is that no city, including the Host, may host more than `max_Dept` departments.
