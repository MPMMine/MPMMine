# Decentralization

A corporation intends to redistribute its various departments across a collection of potential cities. While moving a department to certain locations provides specific financial advantages, it also incurs communication expenses based on the amount of interaction between pairs of departments and the cost of connecting specific cities. The task is to find an assignment of departments to cities that minimizes the net yearly expenditure, defined as the total communication costs minus the total benefits. Furthermore, there is a capacity limit, ensuring that no city hosts more than a specified maximum number of departments.
