# Decentralization

A corporation seeks to assign each DEPT to a CITY. For each assignment, there is a corresponding level of benefits. However, the interaction between departments involves costs; specifically, the communication expense for any two departments is the comm_quantity between them multiplied by the comm_cost between their respective cities. Furthermore, no single CITY is allowed to house more than max_dept departments. The objective is to select the loc for every DEPT such that the total yearly cost—the sum of all inter-departmental communication expenses minus the total benefits—is minimized.
