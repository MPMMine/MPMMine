# Strategic Relocation

An organization intends to move several of its divisions from a central location to various other cities. While relocating a division $d$ to a city $c$ yields specific benefits $B_{d,c}$, it also affects the cost of inter-division communication. For any pair of divisions $i$ and $j$, the communication expense is determined by their interaction volume $Q_{i,j}$ and the cost $K_{c_1,c_2}, where c_1, c_2 \in C$ between their chosen locations. The objective is to find the city assignment for each division that minimizes the net yearly cost, defined as the total communication costs minus the total benefits. Finally, no city $c \in C$ may host more than a maximum number of divisions $M$.
