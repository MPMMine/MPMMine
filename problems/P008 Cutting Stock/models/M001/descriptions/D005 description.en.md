# Cutting paper rolls

The Cutting Stock Problem addresses a common scenario in production and distribution where large stock items must be
divided into smaller quantities to fulfill customer requests, aiming to reduce material waste.

Consider a paper manufacturer who purchases substantial rolls of paper with a predetermined breadth. Customers place
orders for smaller rolls of diverse widths and quantities. The manufacturer’s task is to devise cutting strategies from
the initial large rolls that satisfy all customer needs, minimizing the overall leftover material. Because discarded
material represents lost profit, the objective is to optimize the cutting layouts to minimize the total amount of unused
paper, effectively minimizing the number of original large rolls required.
