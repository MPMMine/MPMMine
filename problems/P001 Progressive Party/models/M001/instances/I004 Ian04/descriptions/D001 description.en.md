## Progressive Party Problem

The problem is to timetable a party at a yacht club. For this particular instance of the problem, a fleet of **8 boats** is scheduled for a party over **6 successive half-hour periods**.

Certain boats are to be designated hosts, and the crews of the remaining boats in turn visit the host boats. The crew of a host boat remains on board to act as hosts while the crew of a guest boat together visits several hosts.

Each of the 8 boats has a specific crew size and a maximum capacity:

*   **Boat 1:** Crew of **2**, Capacity of **6**
*   **Boat 2:** Crew of **2**, Capacity of **8**
*   **Boat 3:** Crew of **2**, Capacity of **12**
*   **Boat 4:** Crew of **2**, Capacity of **12**
*   **Boat 5:** Crew of **4**, Capacity of **12**
*   **Boat 6:** Crew of **4**, Capacity of **12**
*   **Boat 7:** Crew of **4**, Capacity of **12**
*   **Boat 8:** Crew of **1**, Capacity of **10**

The total number of people aboard a boat, including the host crew and guest crews, must not exceed its capacity. A guest boat cannot revisit a host, and guest crews cannot meet more than once. The problem facing the rally organizer is that of minimizing the number of host boats.
