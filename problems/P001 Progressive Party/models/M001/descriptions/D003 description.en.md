# Social Gathering Scheduling Problem

The task is to organize a social event at a marina where certain vessels, denoted as $\gamma$, will serve as reception
centers. The crews of the remaining vessels, represented by $\beta$, will visit these reception centers in successive
intervals of thirty minutes. Each vessel has a limited capacity, denoted by $\kappa$, and crew sizes, denoted
by $\sigma$, vary among them. To ensure a smooth event, the total number of individuals on board any vessel at a given
time, including both hosts and guests, must not exceed its capacity, $\kappa$. Additionally, no guest vessel can revisit
a host vessel, and no two crews can meet more than once. The objective for the event organizer is to minimize the number
of reception centers, $\gamma$, required to accommodate all attendees while adhering to these constraints.
