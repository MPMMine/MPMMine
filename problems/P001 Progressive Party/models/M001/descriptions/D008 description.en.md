# Yacht Club Party Scheduling Challenge

The objective is to organize a party at a yacht club, strategically arranging boat visits to minimize the number of
boats serving as hosts. Several boats will function as hosts, and crews from other boats will successively visit these
host boats for short intervals. Each host boat can accommodate a specific number of people, reflecting its capacity, and
the total number of individuals on a boat (including host and guest crews) must remain within this limit. Guest boat
crews cannot revisit a host and guest crews cannot meet more than once. The event organizer seeks to determine the
fewest number of boats that must act as hosts to accommodate all the scheduled visits.
