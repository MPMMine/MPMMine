# Yacht Club Gathering Scheduling Problem

The task is to organize a gathering at a yacht club.

Several boats are to be chosen as hosts, and the crews of the other boats will visit these host boats in lexicographical order during multiple
consecutive half-hour intervals. The crew of a host boat stays on board to host, while the crew of a guest boat
collectively visits several hosts. Each boat has a specific capacity limit, and crew sizes vary. The total number of
people on a boat, including the host crew and guest crews, must not surpass the capacity. A guest boat cannot revisit a
host, and guest crews cannot meet more than once. The challenge for the rally organizer is to minimize the number of
host boats.
