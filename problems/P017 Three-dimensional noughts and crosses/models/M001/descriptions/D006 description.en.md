# 3D Tic-Tac-Toe

An $n \times n \times n$ cubic structure consists of $n^3$ cells. A line is defined as $n$ cells that are collinear, occurring either along the orthogonal axes or along a diagonal. These diagonals can be found on any two-dimensional slice of the cube or by connecting its most distant vertices.

Given $total\\_black\\_balls$ black balls and the remaining cells filled with white balls, distribute them such that each cell contains exactly one ball. The objective is to arrange them to minimize the number of monochromatic lines, where a line is monochromatic if all its cells are the same color.
