# Three-Dimensional Tic-Tac-Toe

A cubic structure consists of $n^3$ cells arranged in an $n \times n \times n$ formation. A line is formed by $n$ cells that are aligned either orthogonally along the axes, diagonally within any two-dimensional plane, or diagonally through the three-dimensional space connecting opposite vertices of the cube.

Given $total\\_black\\_balls$ black balls and $n^3 - total\\_black\\_balls$ white balls, distribute them such that each cell contains exactly one ball. The objective is to minimize the total number of monochromatic lines, which are lines where every cell is occupied by a ball of the same color.
