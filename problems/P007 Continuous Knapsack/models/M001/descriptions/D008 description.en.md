# Stock portfolio allocation

A private investor with a limited budget seeks to construct a stock portfolio that maximizes total value. The investor
may purchase shares of various companies up to the limits of market availability, provided the total cost does not
exceed the available budget.
Given a set of companies $C$, where each company $c \in C$ has a share availability $a_c$ and a unit price $p_c$,
determine the allocation that maximizes the total portfolio value subject to the investor's budget $b$.
