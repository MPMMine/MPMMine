# Stock Investment Maximization

A private investor with a finite budget aims to allocate funds to various stocks to achieve the highest possible total
value. Each stock i has a profit coefficient p_i and a size coefficient s_i, and the capacity constraint is b. The goal
is to determine the allocation x_i for i=1 to n that maximizes the sum of p_i * x_i subject to the sum of s_i * x_i <=
b, where x_i represents the fraction of shares purchased.
