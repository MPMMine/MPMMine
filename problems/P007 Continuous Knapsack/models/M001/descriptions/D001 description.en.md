# Tank Car Allocation

A tank car consists of adjustable compartments designed to hold various fluids. The internal separators can be
positioned freely to create sectors of any required capacity, provided the sum of all sector volumes equals the total
capacity of the car.

**Problem Statement:**
Given a selection of fluids with varying volumes and values, determine the optimal quantity of each to load into the
tank car. The objective is to maximize the total value of the cargo without exceeding the tank car's maximum capacity.
