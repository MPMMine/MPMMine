# Langford sequence

The Langford arrangement challenge, denoted as L(n), involves constructing an arrangement from two distinct sequences of
integers ranging from 1 to *n*. The core requirement dictates that whenever a specific integer *m* appears in one
sequence, its subsequent occurrences must appear in the other sequence, separated by exactly *m* integers. First integer must be smaller than the last one.
