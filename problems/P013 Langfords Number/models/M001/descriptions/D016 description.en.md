# $\mathcal{L}$‑arrangement

A rearrangement of a multiset that holds two copies of each label drawn from a family indexed by a parameter $K$ is
called a $\mathcal{L}$‑sequence of order $K$ provided that for every label $i$ the two occurrences of $i$ are separated
by exactly the number of positions indicated by $i$. First element must be smaller than the last one.
