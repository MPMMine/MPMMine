# Langford's Sequence

For a specified integer k, the objective is to construct a sequence of length 2k that includes each integer from 1 to k
exactly twice. This arrangement must ensure that for every integer i in the sequence, the two occurrences of i are
separated by precisely i other integers. Moreover, to address symmetry and reduce the solution space, it is required
that the first element of the sequence is strictly smaller than the last element.
